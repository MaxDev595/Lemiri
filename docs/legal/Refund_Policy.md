# Refund Policy

Last updated: To be specified before publication

This Refund Policy explains when a user may request a refund for Lemiri AI.

## 1. When a Refund May Be Available

A refund may be considered if:

- payment was made by mistake;
- duplicate payment occurred;
- the user was charged after confirmed cancellation;
- the service was unavailable due to a confirmed issue on Lemiri AI's side for a substantial part of the paid period;
- another case is approved by {{CompanyName}} at its discretion.

## 2. When a Refund Is Not Available

A refund is usually not provided if:

- the user changed their mind after using the service;
- the user did not use the paid subscription;
- the issue was caused by incorrect user setup, uploaded data, or connected third-party systems;
- access was limited because of violation of the Terms of Service;
- the refund request was submitted after the allowed period.

## 3. Request Period

Refund requests should be submitted within 14 calendar days after payment unless another period is shown during checkout or required by applicable rules.

## 4. Review Period

{{CompanyName}} reviews refund requests within 10 business days after receiving the request and required payment details.

## 5. Refund Method

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method where technically possible. Processing time may depend on {{PaymentProvider}}, banks, and payment systems.

## 6. How to Request a Refund

Send a request to {{Email}} with:

- account email;
- payment date;
- payment amount;
- reason for refund;
- any supporting details.
